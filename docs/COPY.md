## Value props (3)

### 1. Independently verifiable
Every attestation lives on-chain. A counterparty, auditor, or regulator confirms it
against chain state in one click — no account, no database request, no trusting us.
If we disappeared tomorrow, every record would still verify.

### 2. Private by default
To prove you passed KYC, you shouldn't have to re-send your passport. With a
zero-knowledge proof, a subject shows "I hold a valid attestation from this issuer"
while revealing nothing about which person they are. Proof without disclosure.

### 3. Court-fileable
One click exports an Evidence Pack: a signed, re-verifiable record plus a printable
Evidence Record. It re-hashes the original document to prove authenticity and ships with
instructions for anyone to check it independently. "It's in our system" becomes a
portable artifact you can file.

---

## How it works (3 steps)

**1. Issue.**
An allow-listed issuer attests to a check — the subject, the schema, and a hash of the
signed report. The document is hashed in the browser; only its fingerprint goes on-chain.

**2. Verify.**
Anyone enters an ID or subject address and reads the result live: issuer, schema,
timestamp, and a Valid / Revoked / Expired status. Re-upload the document to confirm
it's the exact one attested.

**3. Prove or file.**
The subject generates a private proof of compliance to share — or exports a lawyer-ready
Evidence Pack for a counterparty, auditor, or court.
